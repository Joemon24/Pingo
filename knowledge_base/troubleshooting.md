# Troubleshooting

## Charger Not Charging
For the NG-C65 charger, first ask whether the outlet works with another known-good device. If safe, ask the customer to try a compatible USB-C cable and a different supported device. If the charger, plug, or cable is damaged, unusually hot, discolored, producing odor, sparking, or making abnormal sounds, tell the customer to stop using it and escalate immediately. Never suggest opening or repairing a mains-powered charger.

## Power Bank Not Charging a Device
For NG-P20, confirm the customer is using the intended output port and a compatible cable. Ask whether any charge indicator lights appear and whether the connected device charges with a known-good charger. If the power bank is swollen, leaking, damaged, smoking, making unusual sounds, or excessively hot, instruct the customer to stop using and charging it, keep it away from combustible materials if safe, and escalate immediately. Do not suggest opening, puncturing, or bypassing battery protection.

## Earbuds Not Pairing
For NG-BUDS2, ask the customer to charge the case and earbuds, confirm Bluetooth is enabled on the phone, and remove the earbuds from the phone's previously paired devices list before retrying pairing. Ask which phone model and operating system they use. Do not invent an exact reset button sequence; if basic pairing fails, escalate for model-specific assistance.

## Missing Accessory or Physical Damage
Ask for the order number, exact product code, a short description, and—if the customer is comfortable—a photo showing the issue without exposing payment or identity details. A colleague determines whether any part or product can be replaced. Never promise a shipment, exchange, replacement, or refund in the automated response.

## Safety-Critical Incidents
Stop ordinary troubleshooting and escalate when the customer reports smoke, fire, sparks, battery swelling, leaks, electrical shock, burns, injury, or a potentially unsafe product. Tell them to stop using and charging the product; avoid handling a dangerous item unnecessarily. For active fire, injury, or immediate danger, advise contacting local emergency services. Do not provide dismantling or battery disposal procedures without approved instructions.
