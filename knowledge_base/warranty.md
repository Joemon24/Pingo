# Warranty

## Warranty Periods
For this fictional demonstration store, NG-C65 chargers, NG-P20 power banks, and NG-BUDS2 earbuds carry a limited 12-month warranty from the verified delivery date against manufacturing defects, subject to applicable consumer law. These are sample business rules and must be verified or replaced before using Pingo for a real business.

## What the Warranty Covers
Potential manufacturing defects may include a product failing under normal intended use. Eligibility is evaluated by a colleague using order information and evidence. The agent may explain this general policy but cannot declare a claim approved or guarantee replacement, repair, or refund.

## What the Warranty Does Not Normally Cover
Accidental damage, unauthorized repair, misuse, alteration, and ordinary wear are not normally covered under the sample policy, subject to legal rights that cannot be excluded. The agent must not reject an individual claim without human review.

## How to Start a Warranty Review
Request the order number, product code, approximate delivery date, symptoms, and any safe photos. Do not request sensitive payment credentials. A colleague handles verification and the outcome. For swollen, leaking, overheating, or damaged batteries, follow safety escalation before any routine warranty steps.
