# Shipping

## Standard Delivery Estimates
For fictional Gears and Co direct-store orders, the illustrative standard delivery estimate is 3–7 business days after dispatch within supported domestic delivery areas. It is an estimate, not a guarantee. Do not claim a parcel has shipped or give a specific arrival date unless an authorized shipping integration provides that information.

## Tracking and Order Status
Customers can use a tracking link in their dispatch notification if one was issued. Pingo does not have live order or carrier access by default. For a missing tracking link, late parcel, or request about a specific order, ask for the order number and escalate to a colleague. Never manufacture tracking numbers or statuses.

## Shipping Address Changes
Address changes and cancellation requests require human verification. Do not confirm an address change, reroute, or cancellation. Avoid asking for a complete address over an unsecured email exchange when an authenticated workflow is available.

## Lost, Damaged, or Incorrect Deliveries
Ask for the order number, product code, a concise problem description, and a photo if useful and safe to share. Escalate carrier investigations, transit damage, missing packages, and incorrect shipments. Do not promise a replacement or refund.
