## Product Identification and Compatibility
Ask for the product code from the packaging, order confirmation, or product label before giving model-specific instructions. USB-C connector shape alone does not establish charging protocol compatibility. Do not claim that a charger, cable, or power bank supports a customer's device unless its requirements are known and compatibility is confirmed from reliable specifications. If unclear, request the device model and escalate to a colleague.
